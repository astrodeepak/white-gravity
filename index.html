Privacy notice (business contacts)

White Gravity contacts businesses that use or deliver Salesforce about Salesforce delivery services. We use a business contact's name, job title, business email address and public company information, taken from the company's own website and business data providers (such as AgentData and Clay) and public job posts.

The legal basis is legitimate interest: offering services relevant to the person's role. We never sell these details, keep them only while there is a business reason (at most 12 months without a reply), and email each person about this offer at most twice.

Every email has an opt-out: reply "unsubscribe" and you will not be contacted again; we keep only your address on a do-not-contact list so that it stays that way. To ask what we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, write to whitegravityconsultancy@gmail.com. In the UK you can also complain to the ICO, and in the EU to your national data protection authority.
